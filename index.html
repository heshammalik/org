Spatial Chapter • Life of a Box

The Transformation of Industrial Logistics into Monumental Art

Utilizing recycled cardboard boxes sourced from industrial operations across Germany, France, England, Norway, and the United States, Life of a Box explores the shifting value of materials across social and cultural contexts. Sponsored by the Ministry of the Environment, the project bridges ecological responsibility with fine art preservation.

Brussels Expo

Brussels, Belgium. Featured in "Empowering Europe's Creative Industries," positioning the installation as a core pillar of international environmental storytelling.

Museum of Papermaking

Duszniki-Zdrój, Poland. Exploring the material heritage of packaging transformed into high-concept artistic sculpture.

Museum of West Bohemia

Plzeň, Czech Republic. Exhibited within a historic church museum setting, bridging ecological recycling with spiritual and human development.
