Spatial Chapter • Life of a Box

The Beauty of Sustainability & Global Recycling

Utilizing recycled cardboard boxes sourced from industrial supply chains across Germany, France, England, Norway, and the United States, Life of a Box is a flagship global sustainability initiative. Supported by environmental ministries and analyzed across major European media, it bridges ecological responsibility with monumental contemporary sculpture—proving that everyday discarded material holds profound cultural and aesthetic longevity.

Spatial Chapter • Voices of the Land

An Ancient Lineage of Living Wisdom

Honoring and preserving an ancient lineage of wisdom by thoughtfully sharing integral fragments of indigenous knowledge and language with the global community. From Cree concepts of sâkihitowin (love as the bedrock of collective wellness) to Sugpiaq expressions of Arinartuq ("that which is within deserves respect"), the archive documents how language functions as living environmental philosophy.

Spatial Chapter • روح‌‌نواز (Rooh-Nawaz)

Testimonies of Human Resilience

An artistic exploration of the global Persian community, delving into a diverse array of responses towards challenging life circumstances. Anchored in concepts like bi-gharāri (the active rejection of intellectual stagnation) and Adab (ethical conduct and reverence), Rooh-Nawaz transforms personal memory and exile into monumental visual expressions.
