Philosophy

Letting art speak for itself through the raw chemistry of land, emotion, and memory.

Hesham Malik’s creations transcend conventional boundaries, treating physical material, gold leaf, and structural collage as vessels for deep human narratives. Whether descending into the raw earth of historical fortresses or collaborating with world institutions, the work refuses passive viewing, invoking the inner melodies of the soul.

Recognized globally for defining modern contemporary movements, the practice bridges rigorous academic inquiry—spanning Parsons, MoMA, and the Council on Foreign Relations—with uncompromised artistic universalism.

Ongoing Masterwork

Life of A Box

Transforming ubiquitous industrial packaging symbols—such as Amazon carrier boxes—into profound cultural artifacts. Exhibited across major global spaces including Brussels Expo, the Museum of Paper in Poland, Albrecht Dürer's House, and international institutions worldwide.

First Nations Heritage

Voices of the Land

Bringing together sacred concepts, oral traditions, and linguistic heritage passed down through generations. Created with utmost cultural sensitivity in collaboration with Harvard University, National Gallery of Canada, University of Auckland, and international advocates.

Cultural Memory

روح‌نواز (Rooh-Nawaz)

Deeply moving testimonies of human resilience in words and images. An ethnographic and visual exploration honoring Persian cultural heritage by collecting contributions from global scholars, educators, and leaders at Harvard, Yale, The Courtauld, and beyond.

Life of A Box — Institutional Partners

A massive global undertaking featuring contributions and exhibitions across esteemed spaces including Gallery Carpe Diem (Goa), Amazon (London/Europe), Masaryk University, Jewish Museum (CZ), UNICEF, National Gallery (Prague), Waitangi Museum, Unesco Creative City Fabriano, Nuuk Art Museum (Greenland), Mohatta Palace (Pakistan), Albrecht Dürer's House (Nuremberg), Nobel Prize Museum, and Stockholm Resilience Centre.

Voices of the Land — Manifesto

Bringing together sacred concepts, oral traditions, and linguistic heritage passed down through generations. Collaborating with institutions such as Harvard University, National Gallery of Canada, University of Wisconsin – Milwaukee, University of Auckland, Na’kuset – Native Women’s Shelter of Montreal, and the Endangered Languages Project to create a classic bridge between timeless ancestral knowledge and the modern world.

روح‌نواز (Rooh-Nawaz) — Overview

An ethnographic and visual art initiative honoring Persian cultural heritage and memory by collecting contributions from scholars, educators, and leaders around the world, pooling contributions from Harvard University, Yale University, University of Texas at Austin, Uppsala University, and The Courtauld Institute of Art.
