Spatial Chapter • Life of a Box

The Transformation of Industrial Logistics into Monumental Art

Utilizing recycled cardboard boxes sourced from industrial operations across Germany, France, England, Norway, and the United States, Life of a Box explores the shifting value of materials across social and cultural contexts. Sponsored by the Ministry of the Environment, the project bridges ecological responsibility with fine art preservation.

Spatial Chapter • Voices of the Land

An Ancient Lineage of Wisdom Shared with Grace and Integrity

The primary objective of the First Nations project is to honor and preserve an ancient lineage of wisdom by thoughtfully sharing integral fragments of indigenous knowledge and language with the global community, approached with the utmost care, reverence, and cultural sensitivity.
By bringing together sacred concepts, oral traditions, and linguistic heritage passed down through generations—collaborating with and drawing from institutions and advocates such as Harvard University, National Gallery of Canada, University of Wisconsin – Milwaukee USA, University of Auckland – New Zealand, University of Queensland Art Museum, Na’kuset – Native Women’s Shelter of Montreal, Burke Museum – Seattle, Endangered Languages Project, National University of Samoa, Swarthmore College US, Trent University, Arizona State University, University of Alberta – Canada, University of Manitoba, and many others—the artist creates a unique and classic bridge between timeless ancestral knowledge and the modern world.

Spatial Chapter • روح‌نواز (Rooh-Nawaz)

Deeply Moving Testimonies of Human Resilience in Words and Images

The project titled “روح‌نواز” serves as an artistic exploration of the global Persian community, delving into a diverse array of responses towards challenging life circumstances and experiences that are often deemed sensitive to discuss.
An ethnographic and visual art initiative honoring Persian cultural heritage and memory by collecting contributions from scholars, educators, and leaders around the world. By pooling contributions donated from institutions as diverse as Harvard University, Yale University, University of Texas at Austin, Uppsala University Sweden, University of California, Nevada State University, Jordan Center for Persian Studies and Culture, The Courtauld Institute of Art – London, Göttingen University, Central Asia Initiative, MacMillan Center for International & Area Studies, and many others, a singular expression of shared heritage is crafted.

This initiative transforms disparate linguistic and cultural fragments into a unified visual narrative. It stands as a living archive for cultural preservation and a transformative force in the art world, presenting Persian heritage not as a distant relic, but as a living, vibrant composition born from the power of a single word, phrase, or memory.

Global Academic Institutes
