Spatial Chapter • Life of a Box

The Transformation of Industrial Logistics

Utilizing recycled cardboard boxes sourced from industrial operations across Germany, France, England, Norway, and the United States, Life of a Box explores the shifting value of materials across social and cultural contexts. Sponsored by the Ministry of Environment, bridging ecological responsibility with fine art.

Brussels Expo

Brussels, Belgium. Featured in "Empowering Europe's Creative Industries," positioning installation as core environmental storytelling.

Museum of Papermaking

Duszniki-Zdrój, Poland. Exploring the material heritage of packaging transformed into high-concept artistic sculpture.

Museum of West Bohemia

Plzeň, Czech Republic. Exhibited within historic museum setting, bridging ecological recycling with human development.

Critical Discourse: Recognized and analyzed internationally, including commentary by global sustainability authority John Elkington: "A Market Externality That Rings Your..." (Substack).
